About

19 years making images that speak.

I’m Daniel Cal, a 3D generalist based in Valencia, Spain. Since 2007 I’ve been turning ideas, blueprints and sketches into images that communicate, convince and make projects work.

My speciality is architectural visualisation and vehicle, environment and props modeling for simulators, though I’ve worked on comissioned interior design, product and animation projects for clients across Spain.

The last 10 years I’ve worked as a freelancer, giving me a complete project vision: from briefing to delivery. I’m now looking to join a stable team where I can keep growing.

Process

How I work

01 Briefing
Plans, references, intended use and deadlines. I ask early about the format (still, animation, real-time) to shape every decision that follows.
02 Modeling
Project planning and internal due dates to keep up with the client's delivery.
03 Surfacing
Blender Cycles for lighting in photorealistic stills. EEVEE or Unreal Engine when real-time output is needed. Materials authored in Substance Suite or Blender nodes.
04 Delivery
Client review round included. Final files in the agreed formats and resolutions, with source files on request.
